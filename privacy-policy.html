Privacy Policy

How Ausgetrunken collects, uses, discloses and safeguards your information — for winery owners and for customers, on Android and iOS.

In short

Ausgetrunken stores the account you create, the winery, wine and event information you enter, the wineries you follow and the events you register for. It does not sell your data. You can delete your account and everything attached to it from inside the app, on either platform, and change your mind for 30 days.

02Information We Collect

Account information

You can sign in with an email address and password, with Sign in with Apple, or with a Google account. In every case we store:

  • Email address
  • User type (Winery Owner or Customer)
  • Profile information you provide
  • Authentication credentials, handled by our authentication provider

If you use Sign in with Apple and choose to hide your address, we only ever see the relay address Apple gives us. When you sign in with Apple we also store a refresh token, so that deleting your account can revoke Apple's grant on your behalf.

For winery owners

  • Winery name and description
  • Location and address information
  • Contact information
  • Winery photos
  • Wine catalog information (names, descriptions, prices, vintage information)
  • Inventory data
  • Events you host: title, description, date, place, capacity and photos
  • Your guest lists and check-ins for those events
  • Subscription status, if you purchase a paid plan
  • Event publishing: the store's transaction identifier for each paid publication, and the event vouchers on your account

For customers

  • Wine and winery subscriptions, favourites, ratings and reviews you create
  • Event registrations: which events you registered for, your party size, your place on a waitlist and whether you were checked in
  • Location data, only while you use map or nearby features and only with your permission
  • Notification preferences

Automatically collected information

  • Device and app information (model, operating system, app version)
  • Usage data (features used, interactions) via analytics
  • Crash reports, including the technical state of the app at the moment it failed
  • A push notification token, if you allow notifications

  • Protect against technical issues

04Services That Process Your Data

We do not sell your data. It is shared only with the service providers below, each for a single, named purpose, and with the other users you deliberately share it with — a winery profile you publish is visible to customers by design.

When you register for an event, its host sees your name and party size, so they can plan and check you in. Hosts never see your email address.

Supabase — accounts, database and photos

Supabase provides authentication, the database holding winery, wine, subscription and notification records, file storage for all uploaded photos, and the server functions that run scheduled tasks such as account deletion. This is where the substance of your data lives.

Google Firebase — notifications, crashes and analytics

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging: delivers push notifications to your device
  • Firebase Crashlytics: collects crash reports so failures can be diagnosed
  • Firebase Analytics: collects aggregated usage statistics (Android)

Google Maps

The map and location-picker features use the Google Maps SDK. When you open a map, your approximate or precise location may be processed by Google in order to render it.

App stores and payments

Paid winery subscriptions are processed by Google Play Billing on Android and by Apple's App Store on iOS. Payment details are handled entirely by the respective store — we never see or store your card data. We receive only the fact that a subscription is active and when it expires.

Publishing an event is a one-time purchase through the same stores. We store the store's transaction identifier so a purchase cannot be used twice.

Camera and Google ML Kit — event check-in

Hosts use the camera to scan their guests' QR codes at the door. The image is read on the device by Google ML Kit and is neither uploaded nor stored. ML Kit runs through Google Play services on Android, which may send Google diagnostic and usage information about the scanner itself.

Calendar

“Add to calendar” hands a calendar file to the calendar app you choose. The App does not read your calendar.

Legal requirements

Data may additionally be disclosed where the law requires it.

Note on transfers

These providers may process data outside Switzerland and the EEA, including in the United States, under the transfer mechanisms in their own data processing terms.

Security measures

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Row Level Security in the database, so each account reaches only its own records
  • Secure authentication protocols
  • Regular security updates

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

05Your Rights and Choices

Access and update

You can access and update your profile information within the App at any time.

Location services

You can enable or disable location access in your device settings. Map and nearby-winery features need it; the rest of the App works without it.

Push notifications

You can opt out of push notifications through:

  • App settings
  • Device notification settings
  • Unsubscribing from a specific winery or wine

Analytics and crash reporting

You can limit advertising and analytics identifiers in your device settings (Android: Privacy → Ads; iOS: Privacy & Security → Tracking).

06Deleting Your Account

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time, directly in the App, on both Android and iOS. Open your profile (winery owners on iOS: tap your profile picture), choose Delete account and confirm.

What happens

  • Your account is marked for deletion and you are signed out immediately.
  • For 30 days you can undo it by simply signing in again. Nothing has been removed yet during that window.
  • After 30 days a scheduled job permanently deletes the account. This step is irreversible.

What is deleted

  • Your user account and login credentials
  • Your profile and, for winery owners, your winery and its entire wine catalog
  • All uploaded photos — profile, winery, wine and presentation images
  • Ratings, reviews, favourites and flavour tags you created
  • Your event registrations and event vouchers
  • Event drafts you created
  • Your winery and wine subscriptions, and your stored notifications
  • Your subscription records and your push notification token
  • The Sign in with Apple grant, which is revoked with Apple on your behalf

What remains

  • Upcoming events you published are cancelled, so registered guests learn they are off.
  • Events that already took place stay in the history of the guests who attended, under your winery's name, with their details and photos.
  • Records of paid event publications are kept without any link to your account, so a purchase cannot be reused.
Irreversible after the grace period

Once the 30 days have elapsed and the deletion has run, the data cannot be recovered. If you lose access to the App before you can delete your account, write to ausgetrunken@markono.dev from your registered email address. Full details are on the account deletion page.

07Children's Privacy

Age requirement

The App is not intended for users under 18 years of age (or the minimum legal drinking age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe we have collected information from a child, please contact us immediately.

08Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as:

  • Your account is active
  • It is needed to provide the service
  • It is required by law

When you delete your account, everything listed in section 6 is removed after the 30-day grace period, unless we are required to retain something by law. Crash reports and aggregated analytics are kept by the respective provider under their own retention periods and are not linked back to your account once it is gone.
