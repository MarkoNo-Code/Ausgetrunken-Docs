The app

Ausgetrunken

Connecting wine enthusiasts with local wineries in Germany — a place for winemakers to show what they have, and for wine lovers to find it.

Platforms Android & iOS Market Germany Built with Kotlin & Swift

Overview

Two sides of the same cellar

Ausgetrunken bridges the gap between winery owners and wine lovers. The platform allows winery owners to showcase their products and manage inventory, while customers can discover local wineries, browse wine catalogs, and receive notifications about their favourite wines.

For winery owners

  • Profile management Create winery profiles with photo galleries
  • Wine catalog Manage your complete wine collection
  • Inventory tracking Keep track of stock levels
  • Smart notifications Automatic alerts at a 20% stock threshold
  • Location services Help customers find you

For wine enthusiasts

  • Winery discovery Find local wineries near you
  • Wine browsing Explore detailed wine catalogs
  • Subscriptions Subscribe to your favourite wines
  • Interactive map Visual winery discovery
  • Notifications Get alerted about low stock wines

Design & technology

How it is made

Ausgetrunken carries a premium design borrowed from the wine industry itself — and it is built on current Android foundations, not a wrapper around a website.

Design philosophy

  • Elegant slate black and dark brownish-tan base colours
  • Wine red accents (#722F37) and gold highlights (#D4AF37)
  • Material Design 3 on Android, SwiftUI on iOS
  • Responsive and intuitive user interface
  • Optimised for Android 12 and above

Coming soon

  • Automated stock management Advanced inventory tracking and analytics
  • Location-based discovery Enhanced proximity search and filtering
  • QR code scanning Instant wine information with a single scan
